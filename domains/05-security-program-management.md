# Domain 5 — Security Program Management & Oversight

## Governance

Governance defines:

- direction;
- accountability;
- authority;
- policy;
- oversight.

## Policy vs Procedure

**Policy**
- states management intent and requirements.

**Procedure**
- explains how the requirement is carried out.

## Risk

A useful simplified model:

```text
Risk = likelihood × impact
```

Risk treatment options commonly include:

- mitigate;
- transfer;
- avoid;
- accept.

## Risk Exceptions

Risk acceptance should be:

- documented;
- approved;
- time-bound;
- owned by an accountable authority.

## Third-Party Risk

Questions include:

- What data can the supplier access?
- What systems are connected?
- What security obligations are contractual?
- How is vendor risk monitored?
- What happens at termination?

## Compliance

Compliance requirements may come from:

- laws;
- regulations;
- contracts;
- standards;
- internal policies.

## Awareness

Security awareness is a control.

Good awareness should be:

- role-relevant;
- understandable;
- repeatable;
- measurable.
