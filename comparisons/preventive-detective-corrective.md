# Preventive vs Detective vs Corrective

## Preventive

Attempts to stop an unwanted event.

Examples:

- firewall rule;
- MFA;
- least privilege;
- segmentation.

## Detective

Identifies that an event occurred or is occurring.

Examples:

- SIEM alert;
- IDS;
- audit log review.

## Corrective

Restores or fixes after an issue.

Examples:

- patching;
- restoring from backup;
- credential rotation after exposure.

## Shortcut

```text
Preventive → stop
Detective  → find
Corrective → fix / restore
```
