# Hashing vs Encryption

## Hashing

- one-way;
- fixed-size digest;
- commonly used for integrity and comparison;
- not decrypted back to the original data.

## Encryption

- reversible with the correct key;
- protects confidentiality;
- plaintext can be recovered by authorized parties.

## Shortcut

```text
Hashing    → verify
Encryption → hide
```

The exact security purpose depends on the implementation.
