# Study Roadmap

The roadmap is designed around repeated exposure rather than one-pass reading.

## Phase 1 — Build the concepts

Goal:

- understand terminology;
- establish relationships between concepts;
- create comparison notes.

## Phase 2 — Apply through scenarios

Goal:

- move from definition to decision-making;
- explain why one control is more appropriate than another;
- identify investigation steps.

## Phase 3 — Review weak areas

Goal:

- use mistakes and uncertainty to drive revision;
- revisit comparisons;
- explain concepts without notes.

## Phase 4 — Exam readiness

Goal:

- mixed-domain practice;
- timed questions;
- PBQ-style reasoning;
- final review of weak objectives.

## Study Loop

```text
Learn
 ↓
Explain
 ↓
Compare
 ↓
Apply
 ↓
Test
 ↓
Review mistake
 ↓
Repeat
```
